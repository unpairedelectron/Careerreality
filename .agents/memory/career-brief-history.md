---
name: Career brief history
description: Rules for explaining changes between personalized career reads without overstating weak market evidence.
---

Compare saved profile inputs, evidence values, and interpretation separately; ignore timestamps and freshness-label-only changes. If scoped evidence disappears or becomes unusable, report insufficient evidence rather than an improvement, and always keep source and freshness beside each reported change.

**Why:** CareerReality must distinguish a real change in the user's situation or market signal from a cache refresh, stale source, or missing data.

**How to apply:** Keep brief history persisted per user, compare against the previous meaningful snapshot, and make Home and Plan explanations explicit about uncertainty.
