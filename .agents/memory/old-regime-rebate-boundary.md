---
name: Old-regime rebate boundary
description: Official AY 2026–27 Section 87A rebate eligibility and its separation from surcharge marginal relief.
---

For AY 2026–27, apply the old-regime Section 87A rebate only when taxable income does not exceed ₹5 lakh. Do not infer old-regime rebate marginal relief just above that limit from the separate surcharge marginal-relief rules.

**Why:** Official Income Tax Department AY 2026–27 guidance and ITR validation rules limit the old-regime rebate to income up to ₹5 lakh; surcharge relief applies at separate higher income thresholds.

**How to apply:** Keep rebate eligibility and surcharge relief as separate calculations. Recheck official rules for each assessment year before carrying this boundary into a later tax-year model.
