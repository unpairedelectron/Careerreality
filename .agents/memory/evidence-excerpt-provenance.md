---
name: Evidence excerpt provenance
description: How to label candidate-authored versus copied role text without changing evidence classification.
---

Source labels are informational and must not alter evidence status or confirmed-gap rules. Determine attribution from the excerpt and its source context, not from status alone. Preserve the original line boundaries for provenance checks; normalized analysis text may flatten adjacent lines and leak role context into a later candidate excerpt.

**Why:** Source labels are only useful when neighboring excerpts do not contaminate one another, while evidence status remains governed by the established analysis rules.

**How to apply:** When adding provenance to text-analysis output, retain original source text for attribution and test mixed role/candidate passages in the same input.
