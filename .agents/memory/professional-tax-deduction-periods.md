---
name: Professional-tax deduction periods
description: Official treatment of employment-tax deductions under the two supported salary-tax periods.
---

Employment tax paid by the employee is deductible from salary under both Income-tax Act, 1961 section 16(iii) and Income-tax Act, 2025 section 19. The new-regime rules do not remove this deduction. For an estimate, cap it at the salary remaining after the standard deduction so taxable salary does not go below zero.

**Why:** the official acts separately allow the deduction; confusing the new-regime restrictions on other salary deductions with the employment-tax item understates take-home tax savings for AY 2026–27.

**How to apply:** when supporting both Tax Year 2026–27 and AY 2026–27, keep the legal period labels distinct but apply the employment-tax deduction in each.
