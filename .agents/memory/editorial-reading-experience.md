---
name: Editorial reading experience
description: Article pages use typed editorial metadata for source references, figures, and varied reading treatments while keeping synthesis distinct from reporting.
---

CareerReality article presentation is intentionally data-driven: each story carries external context sources, a typed visual treatment, a takeaway, and verification questions. Figures labeled as editorial synthesis are reading aids, not market measurements or original reporting.

**Why:** Readers need more than uniform long-form text, but visual polish must not blur the difference between a source-backed fact, an editorial interpretation, and a directional decision aid.

**How to apply:** Preserve external source attribution in live pages and crawler snapshots, keep visual/table equivalents accessible, and use the separate editorial rewrite backlog for bespoke prose rather than hiding generated expansion behind a new layout.
