---
name: Career Reality Index diagnosis
description: The product promise and calculation boundary for the Career Reality Index.
---

The Career Reality Check tailors its reflection and tool links to low-satisfaction or neutral self-reports across pay, company confidence, ability to move, and AI readiness. It never ranks or scores the answers or recommends staying or leaving.

**Why:** The user requested answer-dependent diagnosis and routing, while the four answers remain unverified and do not support a score or recommendation.

**How to apply:** Show the exact answers as self-reported, use answer bands only to identify topics for evidence checks, link each flagged topic to a relevant tool, and keep saved decisions narrative without a numeric score or forecast.
