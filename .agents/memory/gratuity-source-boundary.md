---
name: Gratuity calculation source boundary
description: Dated Labour Ministry sources for gratuity arithmetic and the current scope limits of the CareerReality worksheet.
---

The Ministry of Labour and Employment's FAQs on Labour Codes (30 December 2025) describe 15 days' wages per year or part of a service year exceeding six months, state that fixed-term expiry and deceased-employee gratuity are pro rata, waive the five-year threshold for death, disablement, and fixed-term expiry, and state a ₹20 lakh maximum as then current. The Additional FAQs (16 March 2026) state that the revised wage definition applies from 21 November 2025. These dated sources do not verify whether a later notification changed the ceiling, nor do they make a single monthly-rate worksheet suitable for piece-rated or seasonal cases.

**Why:** A generic gratuity calculator can misstate both the amount and eligibility when it treats one formula as universal or presents an older ceiling as freshly verified.

**How to apply:** Date and link the official sources on any gratuity result; keep fixed-term and deceased-employee pro-rata arithmetic distinct from the ordinary/disablement rounding rule; keep the tool noindex while wage-base handling, seasonal/piece-rated cases, and later notifications remain unverified.
