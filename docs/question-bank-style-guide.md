# Complete Interview Guide content style guide

## Voice and trust

- Be direct, specific, and explanatory. State the boundary of a claim and distinguish documented behavior from engineering advice.
- Avoid hiring-outcome promises, salary or demand claims without scoped primary evidence, company-specific process claims, and unsupported frequency language.
- Describe all guides as AI-assisted drafts until independent subject-matter review is complete.

## Terminology and notation

- Use the role's topic-map terms consistently; spell out an acronym at first use where it is not universal.
- Format identifiers and code as code. Preserve units, version numbers, and dates from cited sources.
- Explain trade-offs without presenting one choice as universally correct.

## Item explanation structure

- `short_answer`: precise response to the question, including the central caveat.
- `deep_explanation`: why the answer follows, scope limits, and a worked example where useful.
- `what_is_assessed`: observable capability, not a prediction about hiring decisions.
- Follow-ups should deepen reasoning rather than restate the original answer.
- `answer_tiers.fresher_level` and `answer_tiers.senior_level` remain unset until each answer is deliberately authored for that audience; do not relabel existing prose automatically.

## Depth rubric by item type

- Concept: define the mechanism, distinguish a nearby concept, and provide a small example.
- Trade-off: identify at least two competing constraints and state when evidence could change the choice.
- Scenario/debugging: clarify symptoms and assumptions, propose an ordered investigation, and include failure modes.
- Coding: provide runnable sample, expected output, and execution evidence.
- Design: clarify requirements, sketch components/data flow, and discuss scaling, security, and operational trade-offs.
- Behavioural-technical: prompt for a concrete work example and evidence; do not invent a candidate's experience.

## Editorial selection

Core status and priority are editorial metadata, not a statement about interview frequency. A Core path must be curated against foundational dependencies, public topic-map breadth, and level mix before any item is selected.
