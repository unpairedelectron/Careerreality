# Complete Interview Guide migration plan

## Audit baseline

The existing question banks are structured research drafts, not public guides. On 2026-11-04 they contain 88 source-checked items: Data Engineer 76, Data Scientist 3, AI/ML Engineer 3, Generative AI Engineer 3, and Agentic AI Engineer 3. All five minimums remain unmet. Independent expert review is pending for every item.

The CareerReality artifact has no question-bank role pages, readiness tools, study planner, mock interview, revision system, printable pack, privacy-policy coverage, or associated tests. Existing public routes and tools must remain unchanged until complete pages and their gates are ready.

## Safe migration sequence

1. Additive item metadata only: Core flag, editorial priority, estimated reading/practice time, optional answer-depth fields, and content tier.
2. Migrate existing items conservatively. Existing entries remain non-Core and have empty answer-depth fields until reviewed and authored. Estimated times are planning defaults, not measured reading or practice duration.
3. Extend validation and retain the existing schema, citation, independent re-derivation, and expert-review requirements.
4. Build shared guide modules and browser-only study features behind the disabled guide feature flag. Do not register incomplete public routes.
5. Author and verify role content in the requested order; compute all visible counts from the data.
6. Run content, browser, accessibility, privacy, SEO, and performance gates. Keep failed or unverified gates explicitly pending.

## Non-negotiable review boundary

AI-assisted drafting and source re-derivation do not count as independent human expert review. No reviewer identity or approval may be recorded until a qualified reviewer has actually reviewed the material.

## Estimated-time defaults

The migration estimates reading time from question, short-answer, and explanation word count at 220 words per minute, rounded up. Practice defaults are 8 minutes for concept/trade-off, 12 minutes for scenario/debugging, and 20 minutes for coding/design. These are initial planning estimates and require calibration before public study-plan claims.
