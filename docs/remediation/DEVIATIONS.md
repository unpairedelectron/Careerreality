The amended Income-tax Act, 2025 Sections 19 and 156 confirms salary deductions and rebate limits/relief. 4. **Article claim mapping is incomplete.** The 44-page export has body text and lead/context sources, but the source list has not been verified against each material claim. `CLAIM_REGISTER.csv` marks this gap instead of inventing mappings.
5. **Article decisions are provisional.** All 44 before-scores are provisional (2 score 0, 41 score 1, 1 score 2); all after-scores await an editor-approved rewrite and re-score. No article has received a final “keep” approval, and a score never waives a hard requirement.
6. **Duplicate counts are not interchangeable.** The live-page duplicate audit and raw repository-source audit measure different representations. Both are retained separately under `baseline/`; their paragraph or word counts are not combined. The live audit reports ≥25-word duplicate entries affecting 42 of 44 articles; the other two have no hit at that threshold, not a full human clearance. No shared-text allowlist is documented.
7. **Date-restamping remains unverified on production.** Local article source records use null publication/update dates, but live stamps and each market-record date still need route-by-route human/source verification.
8. **URL strategy is intentionally conservative.** All 44 article paths stay in the staging build with a review notice and noindex. No redirect or 410 has been chosen because merge targets/removal decisions are not approved.
9. **No production release was attempted.** The branch, build, and staging preview do not change the live service or constitute AdSense readiness.
10. **Published SEO verification remains blocked.** The release verifier reports three local indexability differences because article and trust routes are intentionally noindex in staging, plus a live sitemap mismatch: production still lists 69 URLs while staging lists 10. This is expected while production remains unchanged; keep the gate blocked until the owner/editor approves a release candidate and its route policy.
11. **PF is an annualized estimate, not a month-by-month FY transition.** The calculator applies the selected current monthly PF wage basis across 12 months and discloses that it does not reconstruct the partial 2026–27 wage-ceiling change. Month-specific contribution modeling is deferred.
