# Seven-day remediation plan

Dates are relative to the start of the human review; no person is assigned by name.

| Day | Owner role | Work | Exit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Site owner + editor | Confirm operator identity, public contact route, and the author/reviewer roster. Freeze the publication roster. | Approved identity/contact facts and named reviewers. |
| 2 | Editor | Review the provisional 44-row triage. Confirm score-before, unique value, keep/merge/remove decisions, and each URL action. | Signed `CONTENT_TRIAGE.csv` and `URL_DECISIONS.csv`. |
| 3 | Editors + subject reviewers | Itemize and verify factual claims for proposed keepers; distinguish reporting from analysis; resolve repeated text manually. | Claim register with supporting source, source date, support status, and reviewer. |
| 4 | Tax-qualified reviewer + engineer | Check Tax Year 2026–27 statutory schedule, standard deduction, rebate, cess, surcharge, marginal relief, calculator assumptions, and regression cases. | Signed tax method and calculation cases, or a continued hold. |
| 5 | Privacy/legal reviewer + product owner | Confirm actual data flows, retention/deletion behavior, service providers, terms, privacy copy, correction workflow, and contact handling. | Approved trust pages or an explicit continued hold. |
| 6 | Engineer + editor | Apply only approved content and route changes; build, run the strict quality gate, crawl staging, inspect sitemap/canonicals and ad absence. | Passing technical reports and reviewed route decisions. |
| 7 | Owner + independent reviewer | Review the complete package, unresolved deviations, and every gate. Decide whether any route may be indexed. | Signed readiness decision. This plan does not authorize a deployment or AdSense submission. |

Provide a written status update every 48 hours. If a dependency is late, keep the affected route noindex and record the blocker rather than lowering the evidence standard.
