# CTC / in-hand calculator — known limitations

- Qualified tax review is absent; the current rules and assumptions may be stale or incomplete.
- It cannot establish residency, special-rate income, exemptions, actual employer PF treatment, benefit taxation, or variable-pay payout.
- Professional tax is an entered amount; the app does not infer a local schedule.
- No qualified reviewer, real payroll validation, or end-to-end accessibility result is recorded.
- The page remains noindex and must stay estimate-only.
