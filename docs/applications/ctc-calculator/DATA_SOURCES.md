# CTC / in-hand calculator — data sources

- Runtime result: user-entered offer and payroll assumptions, processed in the browser.
- Tax rules: configured in the calculator's local calculation model and described in its methodology/FAQ copy; they are not fetched from a live tax service.
- Professional tax and offer components: supplied by the user from their own documents; not inferred from employer or state records.
- No external salary benchmark or company dataset is used. A human review of the tax assumptions and their effective date remains open.
