# Career Reality Index — known limitations

- It relies entirely on self-report and does not verify any answer against external evidence.
- The fixed prompt does not personalize advice by situation; it cannot infer whether a person should stay or leave.
- It cannot see finances, health, family obligations, manager decisions, or company plans.
- Historical route naming may still suggest a score; page copy must explicitly say it is not an index.
- Keep noindex until route-level QA is complete.
