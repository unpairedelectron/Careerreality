# Career Compass — methodology

The current CTC comparison is `offer CTC - current CTC`; it does not estimate monthly take-home pay or normalize fixed and variable compensation. The offer, move, and stay cards keep salary, employer, layoff, and notice evidence separate.

There is no weighted score, probability, risk band, or automatic winner. The readouts tell the user what is known, what is unavailable, and what to verify. Upstream evidence remains directional and must carry its own provenance and freshness.
