# Career Compass — known limitations

- Upstream salary and company records may be absent, stale, or incomplete.
- Annual CTC differences do not account for tax, benefit quality, variable-pay likelihood, relocation costs, role scope, or personal constraints.
- Notice period is user-entered context, not a measure of switching difficulty.
- The tool does not combine evidence into an objective decision and cannot forecast the result of staying.
- Full signed-in browser and physical-device QA remains open; keep the route noindex for now.
