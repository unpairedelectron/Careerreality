# Company Intelligence — product spec

## User need
Review dated, source-backed company information before asking better questions about a potential employer.

## Inputs and output
Users can search a company and inspect a source record when it passes evidence checks. The interface may show source, date, method, limitations, and a user-initiated watchlist action.

## Non-goals
The directory does not currently produce a stability, hiring-momentum, or employer-safety score. It cannot establish team-specific conditions or guarantee future employment.
