# CareerReality Mobile — methodology

Personalized briefs combine profile context with separate salary, company, watchlist, and live role-demand evidence through the shared decision engine. Missing context must remain insufficient; losing evidence is not improvement. Job-feed/provider freshness must stay visible, and unavailable current data must not be presented as current.

The brief is decision support, not an objective career score or recommendation to pursue promotion, resign, or accept an offer. Every interpretation depends on the quality and date of its underlying evidence.
