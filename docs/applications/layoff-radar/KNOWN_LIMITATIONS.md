# Layoff Radar — known limitations

- Signal labels do not prove announcement status or distinguish a rumour from independently verified reporting.
- A confidence label describes evidence quality, not probability of future layoffs.
- Counts and percentages are response-specific, not a company safety measure.
- No report is guaranteed to be approved or published; the full moderation lifecycle has not been re-tested.
- Keep the route noindex until the taxonomy and review process are approved.
