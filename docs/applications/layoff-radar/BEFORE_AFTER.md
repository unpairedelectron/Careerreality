# Layoff Radar — before and after

**Before:** The headline and summary metrics risked reading like a predictive radar, while a “high-confidence share” could be mistaken for future risk.

**After:** Results are framed as dated, source-backed signals; the page says the share is descriptive and confidence is not a forecast. Error and empty states do not show stale data. The event taxonomy remains a hard blocker, so the route stays noindex.
