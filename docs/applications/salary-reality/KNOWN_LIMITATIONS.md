# Salary Reality — known limitations

- The available role/city/experience choices constrain comparisons; unsupported combinations correctly have no band.
- A source citation and methodology record do not prove a representative sample or a current market.
- CTC mixes fixed, variable, and employer-paid components; the comparison does not normalize those components.
- The displayed band is not take-home pay and does not support an accept/reject decision.
- Live dataset freshness, sample coverage, full keyboard/screen-reader testing, and mobile QA remain open.
