# Salary Drop planner — before and after

**Before:** The title and copy suggested the calculator could answer whether taking a salary cut was “worth it,” while a package-level result could be mistaken for take-home pay.

**After:** It is framed as a salary-change planner, exposes the shared variable-pay assumption, validates every input, and labels gross-cash arithmetic. Salary and expense inputs remain local and the tool gives no accept/reject advice.
