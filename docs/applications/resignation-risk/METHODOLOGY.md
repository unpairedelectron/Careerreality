# Resignation Risk — methodology

This is a rule-based checklist, not a statistical or financial model. Each next step follows the user's selected offer status, notice period, runway, and repayment-clause answer. Runway is repeated as entered; the page does not infer spending or classify it as safe/unsafe.

Contract, tax, benefit, and employment-law interpretation is outside the tool's method. A repayment clause triggers a prompt to obtain qualified review rather than an estimated legal cost.
