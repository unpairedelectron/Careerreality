# Resignation Risk — known limitations

- The tool cannot decide whether the entered runway is sufficient; it lacks expenses, dependants, debt, and timing context.
- The offer status and clause toggle are self-reported and do not verify documents.
- Notice options are limited to 30, 60, and 90 days.
- No current employer, market, legal, or tax evidence is consulted.
- Full browser accessibility and physical-device testing remain incomplete.
