Executive diagnosis

Search Console confirms a meaningful click decline, but not a single proven cause. Web-search clicks fell from 43 in Q1 to 19 through 25 September (−56%), while impressions were 3.5% higher. CTR fell from 1.11% to 0.47% and impression-weighted average position worsened from 6.77 to 14.79. This points to weaker click yield, lower average visibility, or a changed query mix—not a collapse in total impressions. The export is short and incomplete enough that it cannot isolate one root cause.

Do not blame the 24 September spam update for the broader decline. Average position had already worsened by June–August; September’s partial-month average improved sharply from August. The export ends 25 September, leaving only two post-release dates. Clicks were zero on Sep 21–25 (270 impressions), a short streak worth monitoring—not enough to establish an update effect.[1][4]

Most actionable evidence: India accounts for 73 of 96 clicks; the ₹20 LPA article has 1,992 impressions but 0.20% CTR; the CTC calculator has 923 impressions but averages position 28.51; and the Pages export is overwhelmingly attributed to www even though the live site declares apex canonicals. Prioritize those existing demand pages and resolve the host mismatch; neither is proven to explain the quarter-to-quarter decline.

“India’s #1 site” is an ambition, not a rank we can promise. Define the target set—India-based non-brand career, salary and job-decision searches—and track India clicks, impressions, top-10 coverage and qualified tool completions. The evidence supports a focused growth program, not a sitewide #1 forecast.

High confidence: supplied Search Console rows and current HTTP responses. Medium: query/page opportunity interpretation. Not established: why rankings shifted, Google-selected canonicals for each URL, revenue impact, or any algorithm-update effect.

India is the right primary market

India produced 73 clicks from 5,785 impressions (CTR 1.26%, average position 11.19): roughly 76.04% of clicks but 46.95% of impressions. That is the clearest evidence to focus the next SEO cycle on India. The United States generated 3,331 impressions but only 5 clicks (CTR 0.15%); do not geoblock it, but do not let broad international exposure displace India-specific intent.

Host consistency is a priority, not a proven decline cause

The Pages export contains 73 www URL rows with page-row sums of 98 clicks / 12,358 impressions, and 37 apex rows with 0 clicks / 463 impressions. Those row sums (98 / 12,821) differ from the property chart totals. Google documents canonical attribution and omitted/limited table rows, so treat this as an investigation signal—not an accounting bridge.[5] The Filters CSV does not record which Search Console property was selected. Verify the Domain property and Google-selected canonicals for high-value URLs. The live crawl found www still returns 200 while declaring apex canonicals; complete the planned path-preserving 301 to make one host primary. This mismatch is not, by itself, proof it caused the Q1–Q3 decline.

Article conclusion: the editorial library is not thin by word count. Do not add more words or mass-produce similar articles. Check query intent, overlap, source freshness and repeated structure; shorten where readers need a direct answer, and use GSC winners/losses to decide refreshes.
Company profile concern: every one of the 25 profile routes has only 85–93 raw semantic words. A rendered company view may contain additional client-loaded material, so this is not proof Google cannot render it. It is still a strong reason to check the raw HTML and rendered DOM side by side and ensure each profile exposes company-specific evidence, dated sources, coverage/sample size and limitations—not mostly a repeated template. If it cannot be made distinctive, consolidate or noindex it rather than publishing a thin indexable set.

Product/tool pages: the homepage has 43 words, the decision compass 80, and most calculators/risk tools 132–149. These are functional interfaces, so a low word count alone is not a defect. Give each indexable tool a compact, useful explanation: who it serves, assumptions, one worked example, data/freshness limits, FAQs where justified, and a clear next action. Avoid SEO filler.

Trust pages: about/editorial/privacy/terms/contact/sitemap contain 30–36 raw semantic words. Short legal and navigation pages are not acquisition targets; improve completeness only with accurate, product-specific statements. Do not invent legal, retention, vendor or SLA claims.

  1. Offer and take-home decisions: CTC-to-monthly salary by tax regime, fixed/variable, PF, bonus, ESOP and city; connect each page to the calculator.
  2. Employer and role evidence: company signals by team/role/location, sample size, freshness and what a candidate should verify; avoid blanket company ratings.
  3. Job-change risk: notice-period buyout, joining-bonus clawback, layoff readiness and resignation timing; connect to the existing risk/escape tools.

These themes overlap existing CareerReality content. First map current GSC queries/pages to clusters and consolidate cannibalizing routes; do not create more pages until real demand and a source plan are clear. For links, lead with original, sourceable salary or hiring research, not paid-link schemes; quantitative backlink gaps remain unmeasured.

Engagement, monetization and measurement

Current tracked paths show interest in the homepage, the launch-video route, AI career impact, company lookup, the compass and salary reality. The off-sitemap video route materially distorts product/content attribution. Available custom events include tool discovery, company views, compass opens, salary benchmarks, career-index diagnosis and calculator completion; no reliable signup → upgrade → checkout → paid/renewal funnel was visible in the available report.

  • Measure activation: tool view → first input → completion → result interpretation → next action. Attach a persistent anonymous/session ID and a source/landing-page context, with consent and privacy rules.
  • Measure monetization: Pro plan view → checkout start → purchase → renewal/cancel. No revenue, subscription or ad-RPM data was available; therefore no monetization performance conclusion is possible.
  • Use contextual conversion: connect articles to one relevant calculator/decision step, not multiple generic CTAs. Keep evidence, unknowns, freshness and next action visible.
  • Ads: no ad placement was visible in the sampled pages; there is no verified AdSense/revenue dataset. If advertising is added, keep it away from calculator inputs and results, label it, and measure tool completion and retention before optimizing impressions.

The 69.5% one-page-visit proxy and 21.4-second average tracked duration can justify testing clearer next steps, but are not a diagnosis of poor content. Compare cohorts after excluding the video artifact and known QA/referral traffic.
